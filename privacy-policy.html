Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This personal project uses rclone to access the owner's Google Drive account for file management and backup.

Google user data

Google Drive data is accessed only for the owner's intended file operations. The website itself does not collect Google Drive files or Google account passwords.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials and tokens are handled by the rclone software on the owner's computer. They should be kept private and are not intentionally shared through this website.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the owner using the email address associated with this project.
